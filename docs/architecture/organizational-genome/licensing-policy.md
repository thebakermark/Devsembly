# Licensing and Reuse Policy

Do not ingest, transform, redistribute, or use content for reusable commercial blueprints unless its license or terms permit the intended use.

## Allowed classifications

- `public-domain`
- `open-license`
- `government-open-data`
- `customer-owned`
- `permission-granted`
- `internal-original`

## Restricted classifications

- `attribution-required`
- `noncommercial-only`
- `no-derivatives`
- `redistribution-restricted`
- `unknown`
- `proprietary`

Restricted sources require an explicit use decision and may be limited to tenant-private retrieval rather than canonical reusable content.

## Prohibited by default

Without verified permission, do not add paid HR libraries, proprietary job-description products, copyrighted training platforms, private employee handbooks, leaked internal manuals, paywalled compensation surveys, or content whose terms prohibit extraction or reuse.

## Controls

Store license evidence, attribution requirements, source checksums, and takedown metadata. Separate raw source storage from normalized canonical records. Prevent restricted content from entering marketplace packages. Customer material remains tenant-scoped unless explicit permission authorizes broader reuse.
